OQI.ME login theme for Keycloak 21.1

The customized FreeMarker layout and login/error templates are derived from
Keycloak 21.1.2 (https://github.com/keycloak/keycloak), licensed under Apache-2.0.
Authentication templates that are not overridden are inherited at runtime from
the installed Keycloak theme; they are not bundled in this archive.

Original OQI.ME home illustration, styling, and presentation JavaScript are
provided under Apache-2.0 with this theme.
